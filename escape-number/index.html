Your Escape Number is the amount that pays for the life you want without ever needing a paycheck again. The method comes from The Millionaire Fastlane by MJ DeMarco: price what you want to own, turn it into pre-tax dollars, add a Money System that pays your yearly costs from interest alone, then a Peace of Mind cushion. Enter your numbers, then see how long the Slowlane and the Fastlane take to get you there. Works in any currency.

0Before you start

Use the share of income you'd pay in all income taxes combined. The video uses 30%. A higher rate makes the number bigger, because everything you buy is paid for with money that's already been taxed.

1–2Price what you want to own

Picture the life you'd actually want in ten years, not a fantasy. Then look up real prices today (listings, dealer sites) for every big thing in it.

Cottage, boat, tuition fund…

3Turn it into pre-tax dollars (P-T-E-A)

Pre-Tax Equivalent Amount: how much you'd have to earn to pay for it, since you buy things with after-tax money.

4Add what that life costs every year

Owning things costs money: insurance, property tax, upkeep. DeMarco estimates about 1.5% of their value a year. Add everything else you'd spend: food, travel, fun.

5Size your Money System

The pot that pays your yearly cost from interest alone, without touching the pot. DeMarco uses a 5% yield; pick one you trust. A lower yield makes the pot much bigger.

The video's 5% sat just under the 10-year US Treasury (5.24%, Oct 9, 2026).

6Add your Peace of Mind

A cushion for crashes and recessions, as a share of your Money System. You choose the size; the video uses 50%.

How it works: tax factor = 1 − tax rate. A = what you want to own. B (P-T-E-A) = A ÷ factor. C = (carrying % × A + living costs) ÷ factor. Money System D = (C ÷ yield) ÷ factor. Peace of Mind E = (cushion % × C ÷ yield) ÷ factor. Escape Number = B + D + E. Slowlane: yearly savings added at the end of each year, growing at your rate, until they reach the Escape Number in after-tax money (Escape Number × factor), because savings are already taxed. Fastlane: years of profit, plus the sale price at the end, until they reach the Escape Number. Inflation, fees and business risk are not modelled. Amounts are rounded to whole units.

From the video “The Millionaire Fastlane: How Much You Need to Escape the 9-to-5” by The Extraordinary Money Mind. Method: MJ DeMarco, The Millionaire Fastlane (2011). Maya is an example; her home is the median new US home ($410,700, FRED, Q2 2026) and her car the average new car ($50,562, Kelley Blue Book, Sep 2026). Business cash flow and sale multiple: BizBuySell Insight Report, Q2 2026. Most new businesses don't survive ten years (US Bureau of Labor Statistics). Education only, not financial advice.
