1Your edge, honestly

Kelly needs your real chance of winning and what you win per $1 risked. In markets you never know these. You're guessing, and most people guess too high.

2Growth by bet size

Typical (median) growth per bet. It peaks at the Kelly fraction and falls below zero at about twice it: betting more makes you poorer, not richer.

Before you use this: the inputs are guesses. Overestimate your edge and the full Kelly bet becomes an over-bet, where growth falls and ruin rises. That's why professionals bet half or less, and why nothing whose loss ends your plan (rent, emergency fund, the whole portfolio) belongs on the table.
