3Ours First: personal money

In the Ours First setup, both pays go into one joint account. Each of you then gets the same no-questions amount in a personal account. Leave it blank to see the most it could be (everything left, shared equally).

4Agree a check-in number

Pick an amount together. Any purchase above it, you talk first; anything below it, no questions asked. Then hold a 20-minute money date once a month. If a partner controls your money or limits your access to it, a joint account isn't the fix: keep money and access of your own and get support.

The 50/50 gap
—

How it works: 50/50 = each pays half of costs + savings. By income = each pays their share of your combined pay, so both keep the same percentage. Ours First = all pay pooled; costs and savings come out first; each partner gets the same personal money; anything left stays in the joint account. Amounts are rounded to whole units and percentages to the nearest half point.
