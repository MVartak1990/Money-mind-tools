How it works: the part you keep is moved at the end of each month and earns the yearly return compounded monthly. "Earnings spent" takes out everything the savings earned at the end of each year, so the purse stays at what you put in. The 10/70/20 plan pays 20% of take-home pay a month toward debts, split by balance each month, with interest monthly at each debt's yearly rate (APR ÷ 12). "Minimum only" assumes 1% of the balance plus that month's interest, at least 40 a month. Amounts are rounded to whole units. Before inflation and tax.
