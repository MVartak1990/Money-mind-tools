From the video "Rent vs Buy: Who's Really Throwing Money Away?" on The Extraordinary Money Mind. Maya is an example. The model compares two people who start with the same money: the buyer pays the down payment and buying costs; the renter invests that money, and every month whoever spends less invests the difference. Owning net worth = home value minus selling costs minus the loan balance, plus any savings. Step 1 updates Ben Felix's 5% rule (PWL Capital, 2019) for today's rates: property tax 1% + upkeep 1% + (mortgage rate − 3%).
